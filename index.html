Questions

Good to know

Which regulatory frameworks are covered?

NIS2, ISO 27001, GDPR, DORA and the EU AI Act. NIS2 and ISO 27001 are our core mappings. CSRD mapping is part of the Sustainability module. Growth includes one framework of your choice; Enterprise and Corporate include all of them.

What is a log?

A log is a record line or event received from one of your connected sources. Log volume is counted in GB per month, when we receive it.

What if I need more connectors, volume or AI credits?

Extra connectors, log volume steps and AI credit packs are added at the prices shown in your estimate. On Growth and Enterprise, together they never cost more than the price difference to the next plan.

What is the setup fee?

A one-time fee to onboard your first connectors. On Enterprise and Corporate it is credited in full against your first annual invoice when you choose annual billing at signing or within 90 days.

What is the difference between annual and quarterly billing?

Annual billing is paid once a year and covers 12 months for the price of 10. Quarterly billing is paid every three months and covers 12 months for the price of 11, so annual billing saves about 9% on the platform fee, plus the setup-fee credit on Enterprise and Corporate.
